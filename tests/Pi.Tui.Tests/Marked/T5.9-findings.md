## Source view and performance

Tokenizer methods and extension delegates take `SourceView`, a public readonly struct containing the
original immutable `string`, UTF-16 `Offset`, and UTF-16 `Length`. Indexing, `StartsWith`, `IndexOf`,
`Slice`, and bounded `Regex.Match(input, beginning, length)` are relative to the view and do not copy the
remainder. `Materialize()` is explicit and is used only when a token field or a regex-heavy LaTeX
extension needs a string.

Release timings were measured after warm-up as the median of five runs using the plain parser:

| Input | Time |
|---|---:|
| 100 KB corpus-concatenated document | 87.519 ms |
| 1 MB corpus-concatenated document | 705.809 ms |
| 200 KB single paragraph, no blank lines | 54.976 ms |

The 1 MB / 100 KB ratio is 8.06x, below the required 15x ceiling. These are machine measurements, not
timing assertions in the test suite.

## Concurrency and nesting

- One shared `MarkdownParser.Parser` instance was lexed by 16 explicit tasks over 64 non-empty corpus
  cases. Each result matched the recorded token and link JSON; no shared tokenizer race was observed.
- The child-process robustness test lexed 10,000 repeated blockquote markers and 10,000 repeated list
  markers. Both completed without `StackOverflowException`, and the guarded recursive paths use
  `RuntimeHelpers.EnsureSufficientExecutionStack()`.
- The measured safe boundary is at least 10,000 levels for both stress forms. No lower catchable nesting
  limit was reached, so a larger failing boundary was not observed or claimed.

## Deliberate scope and overrides

- `Renderer`, `TextRenderer`, `Parser`, `Hooks`, `marked.ts`, HTML rendering, the `Markdown` component,
  `trimPartialClosingFences`, `MemoryTerminal` SGR work, `mermaid.ts`, and the 81 renderer cases belong to
  T5.10 or later waves.
- The port supports the packet's `gfm: true`, `pedantic: false`, `breaks: false`, `silent: false`, and
  `async: false` path, plus tokenizer and block/inline token extensions. Unsupported flag values throw a
  `NotSupportedException` naming the option. Hooks, `walkTokens`, renderer extensions, `childTokens`,
  async lexing, `gfm: false`, pedantic mode, and breaks mode are intentionally not exposed.
- The pi LaTeX extension is ported as parser configuration. Its delegate receives `SourceView` and
  materializes only for the regex/token-field operations required by the TypeScript behavior.
- `Tokenizer` remains public, non-sealed, and virtual so the coverage subclasses prove the extension
  surface. The test-only parser factory is internal and is exposed to the test assembly solely through
  the packet-required `InternalsVisibleTo` entry.
- No NuGet package, Markdig dependency, Node build/test dependency, or upstream-reference modification
  was added. The marked MIT licence is copied to `src/Pi.Tui/Marked/LICENSE` and referenced by the ported
  source headers.
- The tui parity row remains at its recorded test floor; its implementation LOC is 16,305 versus the
  recorded 16,185, below the 200-line growth ratchet. `.test-parity` and `.skip-budget` remain unchanged.
